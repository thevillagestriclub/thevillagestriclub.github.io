About Us

The Villages Triathlon Club provides a network of information for training and racing activities in a friendly and supportive environment.

Who We Welcome

Membership is open to Villages Residets, including new and returning athletes, and first-timers who would like to learn and prepare for the multi-sport challenge.

Flexible: Want to focus on just one sport (or two)? No problem. Train for your goals.

What We Offer

  • Open-water and pool swims
  • Group bike rides
  • Fitness training
  • Local & destination race opportunities
  • Monthly meetings & mini-clinics

Athlete Profiles We Support


    • Beginners

    • Two-sport (Swim-Bike or Run-Bike-Run)

    • Sprint / Olympic distance

    • Half & Full Ironman distance
