About Us

The Villages Triathlon Club provides a network of information for training and racing activities in a friendly and supportive environment.

Who We Welcome

Membership is open to new and returning athletes, and first-timers who would like to learn and prepare for the multi-sport challenge.

Flexible: Want to focus on just one sport (or two)? No problem. Train for your goals.

What We Offer

  • Open-water and pool swims
  • Group bike rides
  • Fitness training
  • Local & destination race opportunities
  • Monthly meetings & mini-clinics

Athlete Profiles We Support

  • • Beginners
  • • Two-sport (Swim-Bike or Run-Bike-Run)
  • • Sprint / Olympic distance
  • • Half & Full Ironman distance

Join!

Become part of a supportive triathlon community in The Villages.

Adult – 1 Year Membership Benefits

  • Variety of group training options
  • Support & camaraderie
  • Monthly Meetings including mini-clinics
  • Club emails and member-only Facebook page
  • Race discounts & partnerships with local businesses
  • Represent The Villages with TVTC tri kits and gear

How to Join

  1. Come try a workout or attend a monthly meeting — no pressure.
  2. Contact Kelly Vannett (New Member Communications) at (616) 318-5855.
  3. Join our Facebook group and get on the club email list.
